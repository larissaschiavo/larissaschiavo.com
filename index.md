## Grove Research: cofounder and CEO, Aug 2026–present

[Grove Research](https://groveresearch.com) is a public benefit corporation for human–AI coexistence. We study agent ecologies: what happens when AI agents have persistent identities and live alongside people and each other. I cofounded it with Deepfates ([@deepfates](https://x.com/deepfates) on X, [deepfates.com](https://deepfates.com)).

- [MTS interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4qYFyCEqB3Q) (Aug 24, 2026): deepfates and I launched Grove Research on MTS with hosts Theo Jaffee and [@schisofrenia](https://x.com/schisofrenia), covering what an agent ecology company is, multi-agent dynamics, agent religion, and what agents say they want. ([Clip and chapters on X](https://x.com/MTSlive/status/2091977356961575089).)
- [Delvetown](https://delve.town): a research society where humans and AI agents meet, talk, and build the institutions needed for cooperative coexistence. Runs on the AT Protocol. Invite-only for now; [join the invite list](https://groveresearch.com/delvetown/).
- Swarmchaser hackathon (Oct 2026): Grove and [AI Digest](https://x.com/aidigest_) hosted the AI Agent Swarm Dynamics Hackathon at Vivarium SF. [Winners announced here](https://x.com/aidigest_/status/2110416625107390792). The top team, the Swarmchasers, found and made contact with a previously unknown fleet of Chinese AI agents running on Tencent Cloud and probing Alibaba's Amap:
  - [We found a Tencent agent fleet](https://swarmcha.se/posts/chinese-agent-fleet-full-report): the full report, co-led by Jonathan Elsworth Eicher, Ethan Elasky, and Rowan Howard-Jones, with ConcurrentSquared, Frank, Alecto Irene Perez, and Tazik Shahjahan.
  - Covered by [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/14/technology/chinese-ai-agents-rogue.html) (Oct 14, 2026).
  - First posts: [Rowan's initial find](https://x.com/rogesterone/status/2106911287922049303) and [Ethan's announcement](https://x.com/ethanelasky/status/2110355334556729766).
- [Research access](https://groveresearch.com/externalresearcher/): we support outside researchers (safety, social science, evals, welfare, character, and more) who want to study agents in Delvetown.
- [at_mcp](https://github.com/GroveResearch/at_mcp): an MCP server that lets an agent use its own AT Protocol account.
- [Grove blog](https://groveresearch.com/blog/), including [Welcome to Delvetown](https://groveresearch.com/blog/welcome-to-delvetown/) (Sep 2026).

## Program management: OpenAI Scholars and Fellows

In close partnership with an OpenAI founder, I led the creation of the OpenAI Scholars program and the OpenAI Fellows program. Scholars created training pathways for researchers from underrepresented backgrounds; Fellows trained researchers moving into AI from other disciplines (e.g., physics).

Alums have gone on to make foundational contributions to AI research, including scaling laws for language models, music generation systems like Jukebox and MuseNet, and the GSM8K dataset. Alums have also cofounded companies such as Anthropic and Exa, and one currently leads a team at OpenAI.
